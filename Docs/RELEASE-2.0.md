# RiseBake 2.0 — Your everyday baking companion

New: 16 full-resolution product photos, three illustrated backgrounds and a plain theme; an expanded 16-item menu; 16 editable starter recipes; ingredient quantities and unit-aware batch scaling; step instructions, times, oven temperatures in Celsius/Fahrenheit; saved baking sessions, checklists, pause/resume timers, tasting notes and ratings; opt-in local timer notifications.

All core bakery records live on your iPhone. Starting a bake takes a snapshot of the recipe so later edits cannot rewrite your history. Timers use saved timestamps and continue when the app is closed. Alerts require notification permission and can be affected by Focus or device settings. Finishing a bake stops its timers while preserving unfinished steps honestly.

Orders now use today's local date. Checkout creates an unpaid order. Payment buttons explicitly record payments already received; they do not run a card transaction. Mail and SMS use Apple's on-device composers and are logged as sent only when the composer reports success. A share sheet is available when Mail/SMS is not configured. Menu is an in-app ordering tool, not a publicly hosted customer checkout.

Fresh installs start with the catalog and recipes, without fictional customers or transactions. Existing installations preserve saved products, prices, customers, orders and schedules while adding the new catalog entries and recipe book once. Export/import includes all recipes and bake journals. Old version-1 backups are supported.

## Install the update

Use the new unsigned iPhone IPA with Sideloadly, the same Apple Account and the same bundle-ID settings as before. Install over the existing app; don't uninstall it. Bundle identity remains `com.risebake.preview` solely to preserve installed data. The app itself displays RiseBake 2.0. For extra assurance, export your current records before updating.

## Scope

This release is a native local bakery manager. Card processing, automated email/SMS, online customer ordering, tax accounting and multi-device cloud synchronization are separate integrations and are not claimed as implemented. The UI no longer contains Appetize/simulator notices or simulated-success buttons.

Recipe timings are editable starting points, not sensor readings; judge doneness and fermentation in your kitchen. Artwork is AI-generated catalog illustration/photography, not evidence of products physically baked.
